​We now understand what machine data is, ​and how harnessing it can give you powerful business insights. ​Splunk Enterprise is an on-premises solution ​that allows you to search, analyze, ​and visualize machine data. ​Splunk Cloud provides scalable access ​to the tools of Splunk Enterprise ​with minimal infrastructure requirements. ​Splunk's primary features make your machine data available, ​accessible, and usable to everyone in your organization. ​At the heart of Splunk is the index, ​which contains your machine data from sources such as servers, ​network devices, and web applications. ​Once data is ingested into the index, ​it is available for searching and analyzing. ​Search results can be saved to reports, ​which provide continued insight into your data ​and can be used to power dashboard panels. 
​Knowledge can be organized into structured datasets ​called data models, to allow users to quickly visualize ​data in Pivot without having to write custom searches. ​To stay on top of your environment, ​alerts allow you to set custom triggers, ​so you're notified and can respond to incidents ​as soon as they occur.
